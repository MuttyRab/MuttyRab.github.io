How to merge, compress and password-protect PDFs offline (Mac, Windows, Linux)

Merge PDFs, shrink them, rotate pages, add a watermark or a password — without uploading your documents to a website.

Most free “PDF tools” are websites: you upload your contract, payslip or medical letter to someone else’s server, wait, and download it again. For private documents that is a bad trade. These steps do the same jobs on your own computer.

Merge several PDFs into one

  1. Open GUI Hub and choose PDF Toolkit.
  2. On the Merge tab, click Add… and pick the PDFs in the order you want them (you can also drag them in).
  3. Choose where to save the merged file and press Run.

Make a PDF smaller

Use the Compress tab. It removes duplicate fonts, images and unused data while keeping text and images at the same quality, which is ideal before emailing a scan or uploading to a portal with a size limit.

Add or remove a password

The Add password tab encrypts the file with AES-256: anyone opening it needs the password. Remove password creates an unlocked copy of a PDF you already have the password for.

Rotate, split, extract and watermark

Rotate sideways scans, split a long PDF into single pages or chapters, pull out just the pages you need, or stamp DRAFT / CONFIDENTIAL across every page — each is one tab with a short form.

What powers it

The work is done by pdfcpu, a respected open-source PDF engine (Apache-2.0). GUI Hub downloads it from its official release page and verifies its checksum.
