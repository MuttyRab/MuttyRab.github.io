How to extract the audio from a video as MP3 — offline

Save the sound of any video (lecture, concert, podcast recording) as an MP3 file in a couple of clicks.

Download free for Windows

Free · no account · Also for Mac (older Intel Macs), Windows and Linux

  1. Open GUI Hub → Video Toolkit → Extract audio (MP3).
  2. Choose the video and an audio quality (192 kbps is great for music; 128 kbps is plenty for speech).
  3. Choose where to save and press Extract.

Works with MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM and most other video formats. Only use it for videos you have the right to copy.
