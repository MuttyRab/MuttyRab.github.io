HandBrake is excellent and free — and it shows you dozens of settings. If all you want is “make this video small enough to send”, there is a quicker way.

Three choices, plain words

  • Quality: High, Good, Small, or Tiny (for Discord and email limits).
  • Resolution: keep it, or cap it at 1080p, 720p or 480p.
  • Sound: keep or remove.

It always produces a widely playable MP4, so the result opens on phones, Macs and Windows alike.

Also in the same app

Extract the audio as MP3, turn a clip into a GIF, trim without re-encoding, and convert or even out the volume of audio files.
