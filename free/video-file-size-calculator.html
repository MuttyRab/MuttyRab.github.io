Questions
How do you calculate the video bitrate for a file size?
File size ≈ bitrate × length. Take the size in megabits (MB × 8), divide by the length in seconds, and subtract the sound’s bitrate. This page budgets 92% of the size for the container overhead, so the result lands just under the limit.
Why does the resolution go down for small sizes?
A low bitrate spread over a 1080p picture looks blocky. Fewer pixels at the same bitrate look sharper, so the calculator suggests 720p, 480p or 360p as the bitrate drops (the same steps the GUI Hub app uses).
What is the Discord file size limit?
Free accounts can upload 10 MB per file, Nitro Basic 50 MB and Nitro 500 MB (Discord’s published limits at the time of writing).
How do I actually make the smaller video?
Copy the FFmpeg command shown under the result, or use the free browser compressor (nothing uploaded). The GUI Hub app does it from a form, for videos of any length.
