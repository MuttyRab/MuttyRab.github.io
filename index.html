Questions

What does it actually do?

It lets your AI assistant (or you, in a simple app) run 17 trusted open-source programs on your own computer: zip & unzip (7-Zip), image compressor (Caesium), clean up voice recordings (GTCRN denoiser (sherpa-onnx)), send big files (croc), spreadsheet & data toolkit (DuckDB), rename many files (F2), video toolkit (FFmpeg), google photos → immich (immich-go), karaoke video maker (UVR + Whisper + FFmpeg), document converter (Pandoc), pdf toolkit (pdfcpu), text to speech (Kokoro voices (sherpa-onnx)), who spoke when (pyannote + TitaNet (sherpa-onnx)), transcribe audio & video (Whisper (sherpa-onnx)), pdf maker (Typst), ai image upscaler (Real-ESRGAN), vocal remover (UVR MDX-Net (sherpa-onnx)). Ask in plain words; the file appears where you asked.

Which AI apps does it work with?

Any app that supports MCP, the standard way AI assistants use tools: Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code (Copilot agent mode), Windsurf and others.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your computer. There is no account and no tracking. Only tools whose job is the network (sending a file, uploading to your own Immich server) use it.

How does the free version work?

Every tool works fully 3 times on each computer — through your AI or in the app — and Send Big Files is free forever. When you run out, your AI tells you; $9 once unlocks that product, or $14 once for every product.

Is it a subscription?

No. Each product is $9 once, yours to keep, including tools added to it later. If it is not for you, email within 30 days for a full refund.

Why not use the free command-line programs directly?

You can — they are credited on every page. GUI Hub saves you learning their options, installs and verifies them for you, and connects them to your AI with one click instead of config files.
