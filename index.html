Installing

Because we’re a new app, your computer shows a one-time caution message. Here’s how to get past it:

Windows

  1. Download and open the file.
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It installs and opens by itself.

Mac

  1. Download (Intel Macs), open it and drag the app to Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS says it can’t verify the app, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.

Linux

  1. Download the AppImage.
  2. Right-click → Properties → allow executing as a program.
  3. Double-click to run.

Questions

Is it really private?

Yes. Your videos, PDFs and photos are processed on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded to us — we never see your files. (Sending a file to someone, or uploading to your own Immich server, of course uses the internet.)

What does “3 free uses” mean?

Every Pro tool works fully three times so you can see it does what you need. Send Big Files is free forever. If you like it, one payment unlocks everything.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $19 once and keep it — including every tool we add later. Use it on up to 3 of your computers.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

Email or reply to your receipt within 30 days and you get all your money back. No questions asked.

Why does Windows or Mac show a warning when I install it?

We’re a new, small app, so Windows and macOS show a caution message the first time. The steps in “Installing” below get you past it in a few seconds.

Who makes the tools inside?

The heavy lifting is done by respected free, open-source programs like FFmpeg (used by VLC, YouTube and countless apps) and pdfcpu. GUI Hub downloads them from their official sources and gives them a simple window. We're independent and not affiliated with those projects.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Pick your file, choose an option from a menu, press one button. If you can attach a file to an email, you can use it.
