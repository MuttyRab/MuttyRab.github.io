Privacy

  • No tracking in the app. The app has no analytics, telemetry or ads, and does not send your files anywhere.
  • License checks: when you activate or periodically re-check a license, the app sends the license key and a generic device label (for example “GUI Hub on Windows”) to Polar to confirm it is valid.
  • Tool downloads: the first time you use a tool, the app downloads it from GitHub; it also fetches the tool list from this website to learn about new tools. These requests reveal your IP address to GitHub, like visiting any website.
  • Network tools: tools whose job is networking use the network as you instruct (for example, Send Big Files passes encrypted data through croc’s relay; Google Photos → Immich uploads to the server you enter).
  • Purchases: Polar processes your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details from Polar to provide support and refunds.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies; GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • To ask about or delete your data, open a support request or reply to your receipt.
